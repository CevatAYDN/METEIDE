# @mete/ui-studio

Studio view (PixiJS). Visual reference: docs/19-studio-view-prototype.md.

Modules: ui-studio

Today the package holds the run view model the Team panel's views share (ADR-051, docs/13): `applyEvent` and `applyAll` fold one run's events into a `TeamView`, each event id applied once, so the history from `mete/events` and the live `mete/event` stream can overlap. Cards are placed by task status alone (`placeOf`: the Board columns of ADR-051). It depends only on `@mete/contracts` and is a library, not a kernel module yet. Agents are folded too (`agents.ts`): each with its role, provider and model, its task, whether it is thinking and the tool it waits on, from the envelope agent id the core sets (shortcut: the events no module publishes yet (`agent.message.sent`, `review.requested`, `vault.note.saved`) have no effect; the request count of a character card waits for an event that names the agent, since the quota module publishes `quota.*` without one; the animations come with tween.js). The fold also keeps the Studio's signals (`signals.ts`: the roles seen approving or reviewing, why the run is paused, and the workspace's spent quotas), and `sceneOf` turns a view into the studio room of docs/13 and ADR-052: desk rows of at most six, lead roles first, one character per role above twelve agents, each idle, thinking, working on a tool or waiting for quota, and the board's notes by column. `paintScene` (`paint.ts`, `room.ts`) turns a scene into the cozy office of the approved demo (ADR-070), shapes and text in the demo's own colours (`palette.ts`), the same in every theme, in the room's own space 1280 wide that the stage scales to the panel: the wall, the planked floor and the rug, the board with three notes a column (each note keeps a colour seeded by its task id) and a "+N" badge for the rest, the coffee corner, the window with the sky of the user's local time (`sky.ts`: sun, moon, stars, clouds and a skyline whose windows light up at night), the wall clock showing that time, the Vault shelf and two plants (`props.ts`, drawn still at the moment of rendering; shortcut: stars twinkle, clouds drift, leaves sway and the clock's hands move with the idle-motion slice, and the shelf's thirteen books are the demo's decoration until `vault.note.saved` is folded), and each desk with its chair, monitor and lamp, a seated character drawn between its chair and its desk (`character.ts`: the demo's person with body, sleeves, head, hair, face and an accessory, its colours and accessory picked from the demo's six by the role's id, so a role looks alike in every run and events need carry no role kind; a character that works types with its hands forward, one that waits for quota stands at the coffee corner with a flat mouth, and a monitor lights up with the picture of the tool it works with (`screen.ts`: code lines for `fs.*` and any tool the table does not name, a results page for `web.*`, a painting for `media.*`, test rows for verify gates and `gameengine.test@1`; shortcut: the review stamp and the planning bars need events no module publishes or a role kind, and a thinking agent's monitor stays dark since a character carries its tool only while it works), and `marks.ts` adds its name, a bubble of dots while it thinks, a spinner and its tool while it works, a clock and its countdown while it waits, and a group's count; shortcut: a still pose facing left, with no blinking, bobbing or walking until the idle-motion slice), `layoutOf` (`layout.ts`) gives every note and character its anchor, `effectsOf` (`effects.ts`) turns a live event into the effects of the docs/13 table (moves, bursts, marks, dimming, the final jump), `motionsOf` (`motion.ts`) turns effects into tweens (twelve particles a burst, at most 300 alive, reduced motion keeps only marks), and `createStage` (`stage.ts`) draws the room on a PixiJS canvas and plays the tweens with tween.js, both loaded only then; its ticker runs only while tweens play, at most 60 frames a second, and stops when the panel hides.
