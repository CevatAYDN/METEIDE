The Studio room is alive: the window follows the user's clock with twinkling stars and swaying clouds, the clock's hands move, leaves sway, characters blink, typing hands move, thinking dots and the working spinner animate, and a dark monitor shows a drifting dot. The room draws at 30 frames a second while visible and 60 while effects play; a hidden panel draws nothing, and reduced motion draws it once.
