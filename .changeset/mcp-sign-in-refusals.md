A remote MCP server's sign-in in the terminal says why the address you pasted was refused, for example an issuer that does not match, and asks again at once with a fresh address, up to three addresses per connection. Ending the input instead (Ctrl+D) says the server's tools are left out, and the command does not ask for that sign-in again.
