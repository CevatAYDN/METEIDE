Add the team editor: "METE: Edit team", or Edit under a team in the run composer, opens a screen that adds and removes members, sets each member's name, goal, tier, needs and capabilities, gives a member a task, and saves the team to `.mete/teams/<id>.yaml` or as a new team. The daemon answers `mete/template.read` (protocol 13).
