The Team panel has a fourth view, Team. It shows one row for each member of the run. A row gives the member's name from the team file and its tier as a plain label (Thinker, Coder, Fast, Long reads or Sees images). It also shows the provider and model the member uses and its state, as an icon and a word. The states are working with a tool, thinking, waiting for you, waiting for quota, blocked, done and idle. Each row ends with the task the member works on or waits for, and how many of its tasks are done (ADR-089).
