The Team view shows an Activity feed under its rows, newest first: who took which task, who asked whom for approval, the answer with its feedback, blocked tasks, files written, finished tool calls, model switches, checkpoints and the run's pauses and end, each member named as the team file names it. Segments filter it (All, Asks, Work, Run), Follow off keeps the rows in place with a button for the new ones, and a row opens to its event's details (ADR-092).
