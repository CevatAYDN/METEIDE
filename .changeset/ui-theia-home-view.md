The desktop has its first METE panel: a Home view in the left side bar that lists the open folder's recent runs by status, goal and creation time, or says why it cannot (no folder, METE not ready, the daemon stopped). "METE: Open home" opens it and "METE: Refresh home" or its Refresh button asks again; it shows the time of the snapshot until live events arrive.
