The run composer's preferred model, the team editor's pinned model and the Team view member card's model are one picker: Let METE choose first, then the models each role can use under a heading per provider, each with free or its price, its context window, tools and vision, and why a provider cannot serve now; a typed `providerId/modelId` the list does not hold is offered as Use “…”. A pick in the team editor is saved to .mete/pins.yaml at once, and a pick on a member card changes the running model at once; there Let METE choose is Revert.
